Make it, don't overthink it

How to film a tool in under 5 minutes.

These are screen-records, not productions. Your phone, the live tool, a trending sound. Here's the whole workflow.

STEP 01

Open it on your phone

Pull up the Matrix or Blueprint in your phone browser. Turn on screen-record (Control Center / Quick Settings). Landscape-lock off — keep it vertical.

STEP 02

Tap the money moment

Hit the interaction that pays off: the downturn simulator, or swiping the four shields. Move slow and deliberate — the tap is the hook.

STEP 03

Say the hook out loud

Record a voice-over (or talk while you tap). First line is everything — use one from the beat sheets. Then let the tool do the talking.

STEP 04

Caption, sound, post

Drop it in CapCut/Reels, burn in captions, add a low trending sound, paste the caption, end with "link in bio." Tue–Thu, 6–9pm ET.
