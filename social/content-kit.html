Make it actually go viral

The growth playbook.

Great content is necessary but not sufficient — these are the mechanics that turn good pieces into reach and reach into booked calls.

The first 3 seconds

  • Hook on screen + spoken — most watch muted first.
  • Face or motion in frame one; no slow logo intros.
  • One idea per piece. Confusion kills retention.
  • Burn in captions always — accessibility + silent scroll.

Engineer the save & share

  • Carousels get saved — "save this" belongs on the cover.
  • End on a question to spark comments (algorithm gold).
  • "Send this to someone who…" drives the share.
  • Reply to every comment in the first hour.

Turn reach into calls

  • Every caption ends with the CTA + "link in bio."
  • Pin a comment with the link (IG allows the tap).
  • DM automation: "comment CHECK and I'll send it."
  • Stories link sticker daily — it's your #1 click source.

Cadence & timing

  • ~5 feed posts/week, stories daily.
  • Reels Tue–Thu, 6–9pm ET — after-dinner scroll.
  • Repurpose: reel → story → carousel → LinkedIn.
  • Batch film 6–8 reels in one sitting, same outfit-swap.

Boost what's working

  • Let organic tell you — boost only proven winners.
  • Facebook allows the clickable link — best for boosts.
  • Retarget site visitors via Meta pixel (already on site).
  • $5–10/day on your top emotional single beats a cold ad.

Stay compliant & trusted

  • Never quote a rate or promise approval on camera.
  • "Illustrative, not a guarantee" on any numbers.
  • Show your NPN — it builds trust and it's required.
  • Honesty IS the brand — never fear-monger for a click.
